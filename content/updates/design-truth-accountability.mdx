The Sustainable Design Standard honours defenders of truth and justice on November 2, the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes Against Journalists.

**Sustainability needs accountability.**

When corporations and governments cause harm, who holds them to account? Too often, those who expose wrongdoing are punished rather than protected.

Journalists investigate misconduct. Data visualisation turns complex information into clear evidence. Together, they bring hidden truths into public view and turn accountability into measurable action.

Without freedom of expression and respect for human rights, a sustainable future is impossible.

Today, we honour journalists around the world who have lost their lives, been imprisoned, or disappeared for seeking the truth and holding power to account. We honour their courage. We defend the right to know—and the right to be protected.

> Design integrity means refusing projects when there is evidence—or reasonable suspicion—of wrongdoing against humanity or the environment, or violations of articles in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

## NAZA documentary

![Still from the NAZA documentary showing Tel Aviv rooftops at night.](/images/updates/design-truth-accountability/naza-documentary.png "1200x587")
*Still from NAZA.*

**Filmmakers and producers honoured:** Yuval Abraham, Rachel Szor, Harry Davies, Lindsay Poulton, and James Wilson.

[NAZA](https://naza-documentary.com/)—a term for civilian collateral damage—was filmed at night from Tel Aviv rooftops as the city continued below. The documentary examines the systems Israel used, including artificial intelligence, to select targets and approve strikes that killed civilians in Gaza after October 7, 2023.

## Rush: Global Scramble for Minerals Wages War on People and Planet

![Cover of Rush: Global Scramble for Minerals Wages War on People and Planet.](/images/updates/design-truth-accountability/rush-global-scramble-minerals.png "800x1039")
*Photography: Eduardo Soteras and Jalil / Panos Pictures.*

**Authors honoured:** Frédéric Mousseau and Andy Currier, under the editorial guidance of Anuradha Mittal. Research and writing support was provided by Emilio Bunge, Wairimu Gathimba, and Soleil-Chandni Mousseau. Published by The Oakland Institute.

[Rush: Global Scramble for Minerals Wages War on People and Planet](https://www.oaklandinstitute.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/rush-global-scramble.pdf) reports that critical-mineral mining has disastrous impacts on people and the planet. It states that most critical-mineral mining projects are driven by countries seeking strategic inputs for artificial intelligence, data centres, militarisation, and corporate growth and profitability, while only a small portion of new projects is needed for the green transition.

**Relevant SD Standard criteria:** [E1 — Environmental Impact Reduction](/knowledge-base/generated/criteria/environmental-impact-reduction.html) | [EM10 — Ecosystems Conservation](/knowledge-base/generated/criteria/ecosystems-conservation.html) | [C2 — Indigenous Culture](/knowledge-base/generated/criteria/indigenous-culture.html) | [SM4 — Human Rights](/knowledge-base/generated/criteria/human-rights.html) | [S7 — Artificial Intelligence](/knowledge-base/generated/criteria/artificial-intelligence-ai.html)

The SD Standard aims to create a future where design is guided by integrity, accountability, measurable results, and respect for all people.
