The [Sustainable Design Standard](/about) has been selected to be featured in the [2026 Bienal Iberoamericana de Diseño (BID)](https://bid-dimad.com/).

The Bienal brings together design projects from across Ibero-America, highlighting work that reflects the diversity, ideas, and evolving role of design across the region.

Being included in this year’s selection is an important milestone for the SD Standard as the project continues to develop an open framework for understanding and improving the [environmental, social, cultural, and financial impacts of visual communication design](/explore/pillars).

The selection follows the SD Standard’s recent [installation at Abierto de Diseño CDMX](/projects/abierto), where the framework was presented as both a communication tool and a material experiment.
