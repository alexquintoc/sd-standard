The Sustainable Design Standard will participate in [Abierto de Diseño CDMX 2026](https://abiertocdmx.com/) with an installation that presents the framework as both a communication tool and a material experiment.

In 2026, Abierto de Diseño CDMX celebrates its tenth edition with **Futuros**, a program exploring how design shapes the possibilities, challenges, and collective futures being built today. Taking place from September 25 to October 4, the event brings together designers, artists, architects, students, studios, and communities to make design visible, accessible, and part of public life across Mexico City.

Confirmed exhibition dates, venue information, and project details are included in the September 25 press release.
