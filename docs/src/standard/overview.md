# Overview

The SD Standard helps designers of communications, packaging, products, and related experiences consider the environmental, social, cultural, and financial impacts of their work. It can be used from the beginning of a project to set intentions and identify priorities, throughout the design process to document decisions and trade-offs, and at the end to review outcomes and supporting evidence.

## Work through a project

A project is a living record of the work being assessed. In the [Project Workspace](/workspace), you can:

* define the project brief, stage, types, and working notes
* break the work into components or deliverables
* select relevant criteria and apply them to the whole project or to specific components
* track relevance, progress, results, strategies, decisions, and trade-offs
* record outcomes and evidence as the project develops

Projects are saved locally in your browser and can be downloaded as JSON files for backup, transfer, or editorial review. Creating and saving a project does not require a fee.

## Create a Project Passport

The Project Passport turns selected information from a project into a clear, public-facing record. It can bring together the project's purpose, collaborators and credits, components, criteria, strategies, outcomes, evidence, and reflections on what could be improved.

The Workspace includes a live Passport preview so you can review what may be shared while keeping private notes and unpublished evidence out of the public record. The preview remains in your browser and is not uploaded or published automatically; publication is a separate editorial and review step.

## Explore the Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base provides the context and practical guidance behind the assessment:

* [Pillars](../generated/pillars/README.md) introduces the four interconnected areas of the Standard: Environment, Society, Culture, and Finance.
* [Criteria Reference](../generated/criteria/README.md) explains the individual criteria used to plan, assess, and document a project.
* [Guides & Resources](../guides/README.md) offers research-informed guidance for applying the Standard to design decisions, including participation, circularity, sustainability trade-offs, cultural sustainability, AI, and evidence-based claims.

Together, the Project Workspace and Knowledge Base support a process of setting intentions, making informed decisions, documenting progress, and communicating impact with appropriate evidence and context.
